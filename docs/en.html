Frequently asked questions

Everything decision-makers
need to know.

Answers to common questions when evaluating an organizational assessment platform.

Is Sageify for individual consumers?+

No. The website and platform serve companies, organizations and institutions that run assessment processes for candidates, employees or target populations. Participants access journeys defined by the organization.

Can our organization use its own branding?+

Yes. The platform supports custom logos, colors, messages, content and journey sequences in accordance with each organization's configuration.

What assessment tools can be included?+

Depending on your objectives and configuration: interest and competency questionnaires, cognitive tests, games, personality questionnaires, AI-assisted conversation and reports. Not every journey needs every tool.

How are assessments adapted to different populations?+

By adapting participant experience, assessment tools, measures and use of results while considering fairness, validity, reliability and cultural context. Adaptations should not rely on stereotypes or assumptions about individuals.

Who can access the data and reports?+

Access to organizational results is permission-based. How data is used, what reports are shared and how long information is kept are governed by organizational policies and applicable agreements.

Does AI replace professional judgment?+

No. AI supports interactive processes and helps produce insights within professional methodologies. Interpreting and using findings requires appropriate human oversight in each assessment context.
