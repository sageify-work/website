Accessible and easy to use

Accessibility statement

We aim to provide all visitors to the Sageify website with an accessible, comfortable and readable browsing experience.

Accessibility features

This website was built with recognized accessibility principles in mind, including semantic structure, responsive layouts, clear headings, contrast, a skip-to-content link and keyboard navigation.

  • The website offers Hebrew (right-to-left) and English (left-to-right) content.
  • Navigation and interactive demonstrations include accessible labels and descriptions.
  • You can enlarge the text using your browser settings.
  • The site respects the operating system's reduced-motion preference.

External services and known limitations

Accessibility and appearance may vary by browser, assistive technology and external services such as fonts. Interactive demonstrations on this site are illustrative; they do not perform real assessments or submit data to the assessment platform. The product demo uses a local video player with playback, pause and seek controls. At the time of this update, captions and a transcript have not been provided for the video.

Accessibility feedback

If you encounter an accessibility issue, please include the page URL, a description of the issue, your browser and any assistive technology used. Official contact details will be published after confirmation. Please do not submit sensitive information through the demo-request form.

Scope of this statement

This statement covers the Sageify marketing website, not separately configured organizational assessment environments. It describes features implemented on this website and is not a formal certification of compliance with any accessibility standard.
